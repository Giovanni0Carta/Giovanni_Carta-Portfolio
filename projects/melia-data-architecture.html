03 · Business rules

Turning operational rules into enforceable logic

Availability

A room cannot be booked if RoomDay records are not available for the full requested period.

Pricing

Listed price comes from the room-night calendar and applicable public modifiers.

Customer adjustments

Member discounts and agency commissions are applied conditionally.

Booking completion

Invoice, occupation and loyalty points move together with the booking lifecycle.

Business rule → database constraint or trigger → reliable analytical record.

Technology decision

Choosing simplicity for the prototype

Why SQLite

Serverless, self-contained, native Python integration, foreign keys, transactions, triggers, views and easy local deployment.

Trade-off

Limited concurrent writes: not appropriate as-is for a global high-concurrency booking platform.

Production evolution

PostgreSQL or client-server database, stronger orchestration, incremental ingestion and an analytical warehouse or lakehouse.

The best technology is the one that fits the project’s constraints—not necessarily the most complex one.
