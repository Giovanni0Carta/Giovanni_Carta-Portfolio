05 · Relationships

Creating reliable filter paths

DimDateMonthStart1 → manyFactSales[MonthStart]

Enables year and monthly filtering.

DimProductProduct Name1 → manyFactSales[Product Name]

Updates product and class analysis.

DimSalesTeamName of Sales Rep1 → manyFactSales[Name of Sales Rep]

Supports manager, team and rep analysis.

DimCustomerMarketCustomerKey1 → manyFactSales[CustomerKey]

Connects country, channel and customer context.

These are business or natural keys. No surrogate key is claimed unless verified in the PBIX.
