Why this project mattered to me

A marketing question became a way to work with evidence.

My academic background was rooted in marketing and customer behaviour. I became interested in AI not only as a technology, but as a business tool capable of changing how companies understand, serve and retain customers.

To investigate this topic, I needed to move beyond theory. I had to structure a research question, collect evidence, compare groups and interpret the results carefully.

Two perspectives

Business value and customer value are connected, but they are not identical.

What professionals see

  • Faster market analysis
  • Automation of repetitive tasks
  • Advanced segmentation
  • Support for content creation
  • Time and cost efficiency
  • Need for human review

What customers experience

  • Faster access to support
  • Personalised interactions
  • Uneven satisfaction
  • Privacy concerns
  • Need for trust and transparency
  • Importance of human escalation

Data story

AI was already familiar. The real question was how differently people experienced it.

The results move from awareness to usage, then reveal an uneven experience and a clear trust challenge.

Business implication
Companies can focus less on explaining the label and more on making the customer value and limits of AI-supported interactions clear.

From marketing research to business analytics

The research question was about marketing. The process led me towards analytics.

This project marked a turning point in my academic path. I started with a marketing question, but answering it required structured data collection, sample segmentation, quantitative comparison and careful interpretation.

Working on the research showed me how data can clarify complex business questions and strengthened my interest in business analytics, machine learning and data-driven decision-making.

Customer behaviour→Research design→Data collection→Segmentation→Interpretation→Business Analytics

Conclusion

AI can improve relevance, efficiency and accessibility across the customer journey, but its value depends on data quality, transparency, customer trust and the appropriate integration of human judgment.

The project did not make me an AI engineer. It made me more interested in understanding how data, technology and business decisions interact.
